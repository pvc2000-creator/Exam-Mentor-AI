## Previous-year papers and answer keys

No officially published previous-year question paper was obtained. The 2025 answer key and response sheets sit behind the MPESB candidate login (TAC portal); they were not accessed and must not be scraped. Third-party "previous year paper" PDFs exist on coaching sites, but their provenance and licence are unclear, so none were imported. See `research/data_sources.md`.
