# Syllabus

Source: section names and broad topic lists from the secondary sources in `official_sources.md`. Topics and subtopics below are the app's structured taxonomy (`data/exam-config/mp-police-constable-2026.json`), used for question mapping, mastery tracking and allocation.

**Topic weights are an assumption.** Official documents give only section totals. Weights inside a section spread coverage in mocks and are not an official distribution or a prediction; replace them with previous-year analysis once verified papers are imported (`npm run analyze:papers`).

Hierarchy: Exam → Subject (section) → Topic → Subtopic → Learning objective → Question. Every question is mapped to a topic and, where possible, a subtopic and a skill key.
