# Data sourcing and licensing

See `research/data_sources.md` for the full notes. In short:

- The shipped question bank is project-owned synthetic material: 539 template questions with computed answers and 111 AI-authored factual questions that are provisional until reviewed by a person.
- No official previous-year questions are included. Exam-pattern sources are cited in `research/official_sources.md` and stored as reference-only sources.
- Every imported item keeps its source, licence and `allowsRepublish` flag, and enters the review queue.
- Do not import material you do not have the right to use. Publicly accessible papers are not automatically licensed for republication; they can be imported for analysis while their questions stay out of student mocks.
